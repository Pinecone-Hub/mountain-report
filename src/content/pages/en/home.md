1. Glaciers lost on average 273 ± 16 Gt yr⁻¹ from 2000 to 2023, and six of the highest-loss years on record fall within the past seven years [@glambie2025; @zemp2026]. {A}
2. In most regions, glacial lakes have become more numerous and larger (*high confidence*), and mountain slopes have become less stable as glaciers retreat and permafrost thaws (*high confidence*) [@hock2019srocc]. {B}
3. Hazard mapping, early warning and engineering works have been applied to reduce mountain risk, but most adaptation action identified so far has been reactive [@hock2019srocc]. {C}
